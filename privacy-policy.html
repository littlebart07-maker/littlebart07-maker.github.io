Privacy Policy for Auto Video Editor

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Auto Video Editor is a personal app that runs on its owner's Windows PC.

What it accesses: With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube API upload permission to upload videos to the channel the owner selects. It does not read viewers' data, comments, messages, or analytics.

What it sends: The app sends each clip and its title, description, hashtags, and privacy setting directly to YouTube. It does not send this data to any other company or server.

What it stores: The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's PC. Videos and upload history also stay on that PC. The app's developer does not receive this data, and it is not sold or shared.

Removing access: Use Disconnect in the app, or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local files removes its stored data.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
